**Scope.** Screens 9 to 13 and the BPMN model: the sandbox per run, templates, the orchestrator, the 16 development steps per business task, errors and asking the client, the live run page, details, stop, resume, limits, GitHub push and the clean delivery.

**Sources.** screens 9 to 13; `software-elements.md` 3.7, 3.8, 4, 6; skill `bpmn-model` (both levels); `engine-architecture.md` 5 to 8; FR-EXE, FR-TIME, FR-RES, FR-STAT, FR-STOP, FR-EXP, FR-AGT; ADR-027 to ADR-030, ADR-036 to ADR-039.

**Risks.** Largest epic; privileged Docker-in-Docker (deferred risk); 4-hour target; runs must not ask the client except in the rare unfixable case and must wait for tokens instead of failing (D-06, FR-EXE-07, FR-EXE-08, FR-EXE-11 of `requirements.md` 0.4); the 8 further template setups are a later epic ordered by the PO.
